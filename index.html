Origin

Vela is built by a team of robotics researchers from NYU, where rehabilitation science meets hardware design. We build alongside an advisory bench of practicing physical therapists, so every decision is grounded in how people actually recover.

Questions

Each Vela ships in adjustable, low-profile sizing that conforms to the limb in a single calibration pass. The embedded sensor matrix confirms correct placement before any session begins — so fit is never a guess.

Vela pairs over Bluetooth LE with the companion app on iOS and Android, running live data tracking and AR guidance on-device. An optional cloud sync keeps your history and lets your clinician follow along.

Pre-orders are reserved against our first production run and ship in staged waves. You'll receive a confirmed manufacturing window — and tracking — the moment your wave enters assembly.
